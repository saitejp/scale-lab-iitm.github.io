# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Our research lies at the intersection of control theory, estimation, and machine learning. We develop methods for nonlinear dynamical systems, with particular attention to rigorous analysis and engineering applications.

{% include section.html %}

## Nonlinear and adaptive control

We study nonlinear, adaptive, optimal, and robust control, including feedback design under individual actuator constraints. A central interest is connecting controller design with stability guarantees and practical implementation.

## Kernel methods and estimation

Reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS) methods provide a framework for nonparametric estimation and adaptive control. Our interests include online estimation, persistence of excitation, convergence analysis, and kernel representations of nonlinear dynamics.

## Learning for dynamical systems

Research interests include reinforcement learning for feedback control, adaptive dynamic programming, Koopman operator methods, and machine learning surrogate models for control and optimization.

## Fusion plasma control

Applications include plasma density regulation, safety-factor and temperature profile control, disruption avoidance, and allocation of shared actuators. Sai Tej Paruchuri's prior collaborative experimental work includes DIII-D, KSTAR, and TCV.
